# Visual impact rubric

Compelling LED work combines immediate graphic force with enough temporal structure to reward continued attention. Technical novelty matters only when it strengthens what the viewer sees.

## Governing principles

- **One visual law:** Build around a sentence the viewer could infer. A technique sampler has no center.
- **Hierarchy before detail:** Use scale, contrast, placement, edge strength, and negative space to establish a dominant voice.
- **Short vocabulary:** Repeat a small family of shapes and motion verbs until it becomes identity. Introduce exceptions as authored events.
- **Repetition with difference:** Hold clock and source language steady while varying one or two perceptible dimensions. Difference without identity is noise; identity without difference is wallpaper.
- **Intensity through structure:** Increase density, occupied area, contrast, temporal subdivision, active voices, or convergence. Brightness alone is not an arc.
- **Familiarity plus rupture:** Give the audience a stable visual foothold, then violate one rule at a meaningful turn. Fire works as an analogy: familiar hierarchy, concentrated brightness, irregular void, and rapid local change without constant total reinvention.
- **Ordinary-frame craftsmanship:** Judge frames between accents. White hits, strobe, bloom, and motion blur can amplify a strong composition but cannot create one.

## Productive materials

Bold blocks, stripes, rails, targets, masks, hard fields, glyphs, limited text, and simple repeated motifs survive physical spacing and distance. Fine procedural texture should support those forms rather than compete with them. Rainbows are valid only when spectral order is the premise; continuous hue cycling is not a default source of development.

## Common failure modes

- every pixel remains active, leaving no room to build;
- all colors and shapes carry equal weight;
- smooth scalar drift replaces authored states;
- new effects appear whenever energy should increase;
- strobe or full white substitutes for composition;
- complexity is visible mainly to the programmer;
- palette changes become constant novelty and lose consequence;
- the peak has nowhere to go because the opening already spends everything.

## Score the direction

Score each dimension from 0 to 3. Treat any score below 2 as a redesign target.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Premise | absent | generic | clear | unmistakable |
| Hierarchy | flat | inconsistent | legible | commanding |
| Palette roles | arbitrary | attractive only | functional | structural |
| Motion grammar | unrelated | loosely related | coherent | signature |
| Temporal form | loop/drift | minor variation | authored sections | inevitable arc |
| Restraint | everything on | occasional space | deliberate withholding | tension through absence |
| Ordinary frame | unreadable | dependent on accents | sound | iconic |
| Surprise | random | decorative | meaningful turn | memorable rupture and return |
